Wave Planner is a free, fan-made tool for coordinating a kingdom in the online game Utopia, together with a Discord bot that collects the game's automated dragon event messages. Using either means accepting what follows.

What the bot does

Once invited to a server, the bot reads one designated channel and stores the game's automated DRAGON event lines so the planner can rank who has funded or slain a dragon. It posts occasional reminders naming provinces that have not yet taken part. It does not read other channels or direct messages. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored.
